Questions

Frequently asked.

Can I upload a PDF CV?
Yes. You can upload PDF, DOCX, or paste text directly. For the fastest result, a plain text paste or summary works well — we read what you've built, not just file formatting.
How accurate is the evidence score?
The score is a composite of keyword signal, portfolio artifact match, and experience fit against hiring patterns for your target role. It's a directional indicator — not a guarantee — but it's far more specific than keyword matching. The gap artifacts are what matter most.
Does this work outside AI and research roles?
Yes. Paste any job description — strategy, policy, evaluation, product, consulting, or any domain. FutuMatch extracts the proof signals that role demands and maps them against your CV. The output is always role-specific.
Can I use a LinkedIn URL instead of a CV?
LinkedIn URL support is coming. For now, copy and paste your LinkedIn summary or experience section — it works just as well as a formatted CV.
What happens after the analysis?
You get your Gap Map free. Unlock the Idealized Profile™, Artifact Roadmap, and Immediate Offer Strategy with credits. Each output is built from your specific CV and target role — not templates. Credits never expire.
Is my CV stored or used to train AI?
No. Your CV is used only to generate your analysis and is never used to train AI models. Ever.
