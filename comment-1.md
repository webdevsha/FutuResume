# FUTURESUME
# Landing Page Refinement & Conversion Strategy
### Version 2.0
### From "Resume Checker" → "Career Proof Intelligence Platform"

---

# Vision

The landing page should not feel like another AI resume tool.

It should immediately communicate that Futuresume is an entirely new category.

Not:

> Resume Optimization

Not:

> ATS Checker

Not:

> Career Coach

Instead:

> Career Proof Intelligence Platform

Futuresume diagnoses the gap between who someone is today and the portfolio identity they need to become visible for tomorrow's opportunities.

The product is not about fixing resumes.

It is about building proof.

---

# Core Philosophy

Every page, sentence, animation and interaction should reinforce one belief.

> Hiring rewards proof, not potential.

or

> Recruiters don't hire skills.
> They hire evidence.

Everything else should support this message.

Futuresume is the world's first Career Proof Intelligence Platform.

The product does not optimise applications.
